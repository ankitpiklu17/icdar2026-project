Abstract

Optical Music Recognition (OMR) systems have traditionally focused on homogeneous music notation, limiting their applicability to real-world scenarios where multiple notation styles coexist within the same document. This paper presents a Multi-Modal Heterogeneous Music Notation Recognition Framework that unifies detection, structural understanding, and symbolic reconstruction into a single pipeline. Our approach integrates heterogeneous notation detection, graph-based structural reasoning, and MusicXML generation to establish a direct symbolic-to-immersive mapping for Extended Reality (XR) applications. Furthermore, we introduce the SWARA dataset containing annotated percussion notation and combine it with DeepScoresV2 to create a heterogeneous benchmark for training and evaluation. Extensive experiments demonstrate the robustness of the proposed framework across multiple notation styles while providing a foundation for immersive music learning, digital preservation, and intelligent music interaction.
