Summary

Optical Music Recognition (OMR) systems have traditionally focused on homogeneous music notation, limiting their applicability to real-world scenarios where multiple notation styles coexist within the same document. This work introduces SWARA (Sheet music With Augmented Recognition for metAverse), a unified framework for heterogeneous music notation recognition and real-time symbolic-to-immersive mapping. Our contributions include: (1) the first annotated percussion sheet music dataset integrated with piano notation, (2) a unified recognition pipeline capable of detecting heterogeneous music symbols and generating structured MusicXML, (3) a semantic Symbol-to-XR mapping framework with a novel evaluation metric for immersive music interaction, and (4) a collaborative metaverse environment that enables multi-user music learning and performance. By bridging the gap between static sheet music and interactive Extended Reality (XR), SWARA establishes a benchmark and an end-to-end Human-Document Interaction (HDI) framework for real-time heterogeneous music understanding.

Experiments & Results

All models were trained for 50 epochs on an NVIDIA RTX A4500 GPU using the Adam optimizer. The unified heterogeneous dataset consists of 49 musical symbol classes from piano and percussion notation with an 80:10:10 train-validation-test split. We evaluate both the proposed SWARA Staff Module for staff-line segmentation and multiple object detectors for heterogeneous musical symbol detection.
