Multi-Modal Heterogeneous Music Notation Recognition from Documents

A Framework for Real-Time Symbolic-to-Immersive Mapping

Ankit Sinha1 · Atanu Saha1 · Rahul Kumar Ray1 · Chiranjoy Chattopadhyay1

1School of Computing and Data Sciences, FLAME University, Pune, India

Paper Poster Code Dataset Video

Overview

A unified framework that transforms heterogeneous music notation documents into interactive immersive musical experiences through automatic MusicXML generation and XR visualization.

Summary

Motivation

Existing Optical Music Recognition (OMR) systems mainly focus on Western melodic notation and provide limited support for heterogeneous music scores.

Challenge

Develop a unified OMR framework capable of recognizing heterogeneous music notation, generating MusicXML, and enabling immersive XR-based music learning.

SWARA Framework

We propose SWARA (Sheet music With Augmented Recognition for metAverse), a unified heterogeneous music notation recognition framework for real-time symbolic-to-immersive mapping.

Contributions

  • First annotated percussion sheet music dataset.
  • Unified heterogeneous OMR framework.
  • MusicXML generation pipeline.
  • Real-time XR interaction.
  • Collaborative musical metaverse.
