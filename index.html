Summary

  • Motivation: Existing Optical Music Recognition (OMR) systems primarily focus on Western melodic notation and offer limited support for heterogeneous music scores.
  • Challenge: Developing a single OMR framework capable of accurately recognizing heterogeneous music notations including piano and percussion notation while enabling immersive interaction.
  • SWARA Framework: We propose SWARA (Sheet music With Augmented Recognition for metAverse), a unified framework for heterogeneous music notation recognition and real-time symbolic-to-immersive mapping.
  • Contributions: Introduce the first annotated percussion sheet music dataset, a unified heterogeneous OMR model, a Symbol-to-XR mapping framework, and a collaborative musical metaverse.
  • Impact: SWARA advances Human-Document Interaction (HDI) by transforming static sheet music into interactive, immersive music learning experiences.

Proposed Framework

The proposed SWARA framework performs end-to-end heterogeneous music notation recognition by transforming sheet music into structured MusicXML and real-time immersive XR interaction. The pipeline integrates preprocessing, staff-line removal, strip extraction, heterogeneous symbol detection, semantic reconstruction, and MusicXML generation within a unified Human-Document Interaction (HDI) framework.
